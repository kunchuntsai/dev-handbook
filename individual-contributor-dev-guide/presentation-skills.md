# Presentation Skills - 簡報課

Course: <https://sat.cool/classroom/18>

三個重點: 策略(Strategy), 設計(Design), 呈現(Deliver)

## Strategy

- Who are they: 聽眾是誰
- How to impress: 如何讓聽眾留下印象
- Why me: 只有我可以做這件事的原因
- What to get: 講完這次的報告(我)想獲得的是什麼

## Design

- 演講的結構: 開場、主題、結尾
- 如何讓聽眾持續聽你講話: 對他們有用，有趣

### PBP: problem based presentation

- Whatever you do should be linked to a problem
- 問題為主要演講: 如何解決他們的問題

### Opening

### 1. Attracting audiences

- Raising a question, reveal a problem; link it to them
- Give a tremendous solution; link it to them
- Arouse curiosity/appeal to them
- Previous successful experience/results
- Use a short video clip

### 2. Define your public persona (人設)

- Content v.s. reaction
  - Serious persona then talk a joke (?!)
- Will be defined in the first 3~5 min

### Tips: Conclusion

- Result and conclusion should be delivered in the opening
- Highlight the important thing first, then people will focus on the content
- Content: A -> B -> C -> D (conclusion) (禁: 流水帳)
  - Deliver as: D; A -> B -> C

### Summary of the opening

1. brief intro
2. opening skills
3. describe the solution to the problems
4. show the outline, give audience a good expectation (e.g. content/timeframe)

Note: 1~4 within 3~5 min, less than 10% of your presentation

### Content/Body

- Construct a logical structure for the audiences
- Two layers would be good

P1

- P1.1
- P1.2
- P1.2.1 (<-- too many layers)

P2

- p2.1
- P2.2

### Ending

1. Recapitulate/highlight the main ideas
2. Address the conclusion again, less than 3 slides
3. Call for actions
4. Put a quote

### Content; Type1 - Story (故事理念)

- Scenario
  - 新創募資
  - 公益勸募
  - 改變思維，價值觀溝通
- Structure
  - 故事
  - 成果/體悟/道理
  - Action
- 適用場景 - 即席演講
  - Have 5 prepared stories

## Delivery

- The most important factor: Passion
- Tell a story like the very first time every time
- How to keep your passion? Engagement/devotement

### Make them understand

- Use their (audiences) words
- Strategy: investigation the audiences

### Tell a story

- Tell a story with own personal experience
- Telling a story with shaping a image of it
- Describe a picture in your talk (話中有畫)

### Analogy/Convert

- Use a simple example to explain complex concepts

### Humor

- Make a joke on himself/herself
- Fallacy, 邏輯誤導，導到一個歪掉的結論，再拉回來
- Use Meme
- Avoiding
  - 自嘲，不是自捧
  - Bias: 性別，顏值，種族，階級，工作相關玩笑
  - 不攻擊別人
- 開局要勇敢 (建立人設，至少試三次)

### Make questions

- Let the slide title talk
- The questions are the audiences want to ask
- Use the questions to make the flow continuously
  - Know what the audience think
  - Content should be clear and simple, then you can foresee the thoughts easily

### Tell a story, create the impact

- Like cooking: appetizer, main course, flavor, dessert, surprise
- Appetizer
  - background, context
  - prepare for the main character
  - setup a good circumstance for the main character
- Main course
  - main structure of the story
  - 變應交替，變局/應對
- Flavor
  - 融入角色
  - not only his/her experience but his/her emotion
- Dessert
  - 置入性行銷
  - what's the purpose behind
- Surprise
  - Give a punch line if any

### Body language

- Stance, posture
  - Santa pose, "三七步"
  - Be stable, don't move around, that will distract your audiences
  - Cross-hands
  - self-awareness
- Gesture
  - follow your sentences

### Eye contact

- Be even: scan every audiences
  - Exception: only on the decision makers
- Keep 2 seconds, or it will make people feel unconfident
- With courage, to make eye contacts
- Listen
- 眼神柔軟/溫暖

### Practice

- Say it out loud, present in front of a mirror
  - 默唸是沒有用的
- Pointers
  - don't circle the whole parts/sentences
  - just stay in the beginning, guide audiences' eyes
